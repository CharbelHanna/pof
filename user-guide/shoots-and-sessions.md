# Shoots and sessions

How a job is put together.

## The three levels

**A shoot** is one job. "Vogue Italia editorial", "Harbour at dawn", "Chen
family portraits". This is the main thing you work with, and everything else
hangs off it — the shot list, the checklists, the crew, the gear, the
references.

**A session** is one block of shooting within that job. A shoot that happens in
a single morning has one session. A three-day trip has three, or more. Each
session has its own date, time, length and location.

**A campaign** is optional, and groups related shoots together. A client who
books you for four separate shoots across a season is a campaign. A single
sunrise on a cliff is not — and the app never makes you invent one. Most
shoots sit on their own quite happily.

You will find all three in the menu: **Shoots**, **Campaigns**, **Agenda**.

## Creating a shoot

On the **Shoots** page, type a name into **Shoot name**, choose a type, and
press **Add shoot**.

Open it by tapping its name in the list.

## Shoot types

Choosing a type changes which extra fields you are asked for, because what
matters varies enormously between kinds of work.

| Type          | For                                                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Portrait**  | People — headshots, personal branding, portfolio work.               |
| **Editorial** | Commissioned work for a publication or brand story.                  |
| **Landscape** | Outdoor and location work where light and access decide the day.     |
| **Product**   | Catalogue, packshot and still-life work, usually at volume.          |
| **Boudoir**   | Intimate portraiture, where privacy and comfort shape the whole day. |

The extra fields follow from that. A Portrait shoot asks how many subjects,
and about wardrobe, hair and makeup. An Editorial shoot asks which
publication, the issue date, the art director and how many looks. A Landscape
shoot asks about the walk in, the tide and whether a permit is needed.

Everything that matters across all types — dates, status, location, delivery
date — is asked of every shoot regardless. The type only adds the handful of
things that are specific to that kind of work.

You can change the type later. The fields change with it.

## Sessions

Add a session from inside the shoot. You give it:

- **Session name** — "Day one", "Golden hour at the quay", "Studio".
- **Session start** — the date and time it begins.
- **Session length in hours**.
- **Session timezone** — which matters more than it looks. Times are shown in
  the session's own zone, so a shoot in Dubrovnik planned from London shows
  the times you will actually experience, not the ones your laptop thinks.

Sessions appear on the **Agenda** page alongside everything else you have
coming up.

## What the statuses mean

### Shoot statuses

- **Draft** — you are still sketching it out. Nothing is agreed.
- **Planned** — it is real and in the diary, but not yet locked in with
  everyone involved.
- **Confirmed** — the client, the crew and the date are all agreed. This is
  the one that means "do not double-book this day".
- **Shooting** — it is happening now.
- **Completed** — the shooting is done.
- **Cancelled** — it is not happening. Kept rather than deleted, because a
  cancelled shoot is a fact about your year worth being able to look back on.

### Session statuses

The same, minus Draft: **Planned**, **Confirmed**, **Shooting**,
**Completed**, **Cancelled**.

A shoot and its sessions can legitimately disagree — a Confirmed shoot can
have one session completed and the next still planned. Nothing is calculated
automatically from the other, because only you know what has actually been
agreed.

## Things worth knowing

- A shoot does not need a campaign, a client or a location to exist. Name it
  and fill the rest in later.
- Deleting a shoot removes the things attached to it. Be sure.
- A session's date and time cannot be changed once it is created in this
  version. If a shoot moves, you will need to add a new session and delete the
  old one — which does mean losing anything attached to it, so do it before
  you build the day out.
