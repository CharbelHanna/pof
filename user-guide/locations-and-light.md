# Locations and light

Where you are shooting, and when the light will be worth having.

## Adding a location

On the shoot page, use **Search for a place** — type a name and pick from the
results. "Glen Coe", "Dubrovnik old town", a street address.

Once a place is saved it stays in your list, so somewhere you shoot often is
typed once and picked thereafter.

A saved location is a name and an address. In this version there is nowhere to
record the things you only learn by going — the gate code, which car park
actually works, whether anyone asked you for a permit. Keep those wherever you
keep them now.

> **Note:** a location has to be found by searching. Somewhere the search does
> not know — a private studio, a client's house, a field with a gate reference
> — cannot yet be added by typing the address in yourself.

## Several stops in one session

A session can have more than one location, in order.

This is how a real shooting day usually goes: start at the harbour, move up to
the castle for midday, finish on the headland for sunset. Each stop is its own
place with its own notes, rather than one vague location covering the lot.

## Sun times

Once a session has a date and a location, the app works out the light for that
exact spot on that exact day:

- **Sunrise** and **Sunset**
- **Golden** hour — the warm, low, directional light just after sunrise and
  just before sunset
- **Blue** hour — the cool, even light before sunrise and after sunset

These are calculated rather than fetched, which has two consequences worth
knowing. They are exact — astronomy, not an estimate — and they work for any
date you like, including one two years away. Planning an August trip next
winter will give you that August's light to the minute.

Times are shown in the session's own timezone. A Dubrovnik session planned
from London shows Dubrovnik's sunrise, not London's.

## What this does not do

**There is no weather.** Sun times and weather are different problems: the sun
is predictable centuries ahead, weather is not predictable beyond about a
fortnight. Nothing in this version forecasts cloud, rain or wind, and you
should keep using whatever you use today for that.

## Things worth knowing

- Sun times need coordinates, which come from the search result. A session
  with no location has no light information.
- If a session has several stops, the sun times shown are for the first one.
  On a day where your stops are far apart — different valleys, different sides
  of a mountain — the real difference between them can be larger than it
  looks.
- There is no map view in this version. Locations are a name, an address and
  their notes.
