# References

The images you are working from — the mood board for the shoot.

Found on the shoot page under **References**.

## Adding images

Press **Add references**, or drag images onto the area below it.

On a phone this opens your camera roll, which is usually where references
live by the time you are planning — screenshots, saved posts, pictures of a
location you took when you scouted it.

Accepted formats are **JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC**. HEIC matters if you are on
an iPhone, since that is what it shoots by default.

You can select several at once.

## What happens after you add one

An image takes a moment to appear properly. It is uploaded first, then resized
in the background into the versions the app actually displays, so that a board
of thirty references does not download thirty full-resolution files to your
phone.

This is why a picture may briefly show as not quite ready. Give it a few
seconds.

## Arranging the board

Drag the **Drag** handle on a tile to move it. The order is yours to set —
grouped by look, or by location, or in the order you want to work through
them.

Keyboard: click the handle and use the **arrow keys**.

The arrangement saves as you go.

## Looking at one properly

Tap a tile to open it large. Close it with the **Close** button.

## Removing one

**Remove**, on the tile.

## Things worth knowing

- References belong to the whole shoot, not to a single session.
- The images are private to you, like everything else in your account.
- There is no way to pull images in from Pinterest or a web address in this
  version — they have to be files on the device you are using. Saving a
  picture to your phone first is the way round it.
- Attaching a specific reference to a specific _shot_, rather than the shoot
  as a whole, is not available in this version.
