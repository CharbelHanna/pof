# Shot lists

The frames you intend to come away with.

A shoot is booked and a session is scheduled, but a shot is the thing that
either got taken or did not — and it is what a client asks about afterwards.
The shot list is the plan for the day, in the order you mean to work through
it.

## Adding shots

On the shoot page, under **Shots**, type into **Shot title** and press **Add
shot**.

Titles only, deliberately. Shot ideas usually arrive in a rush — six at once,
in the car, in the middle of a conversation — and stopping to fill in a form
each time means losing the next five. Get them down, flesh them out later.

## Filling in the detail

Press **Details** beside a shot to open it. You get:

- **Framing** — Wide, Medium, Close-up, Over the shoulder, Flat lay, and so
  on. The box suggests common ones but you can type anything; a landscape
  photographer and a product photographer do not share a vocabulary here.
- **Subjects** — who or what is in the frame.
- **Props** — what needs to be in shot or to hand.
- **Description** — anything that will not fit in the above. "Shoot into the
  low sun." "Hands only, no face."
- **Session** — which day this shot belongs to, if the shoot has more than
  one. The default is **Whole shoot**, meaning you have not decided yet, which
  is usually the truth when the idea first arrives.

Changes save when you click away from a box.

Press **Less** to close the detail again.

## The running order

The order of the list is the plan for the day, so you arrange it yourself
rather than it being sorted for you.

Drag the **Drag** handle at the left of a row to move a shot. The list
rearranges as you go and saves when you let go.

If you would rather not drag — or you are on a keyboard — click the **Drag**
handle and use the **up and down arrow keys**. It moves one place per press
and saves as it goes.

## Marking what you got

Each shot has a status:

- **planned** — intended, not yet attempted.
- **captured** — in the can.
- **skipped** — deliberately abandoned. The light went, the location was shut,
  the client changed their mind.

You can set these from the dropdown here, but on the day it is far easier to
use [on-set mode](on-set-mode.md), which is built for exactly that and has
buttons big enough to hit while holding a camera.

Skipped is worth using properly rather than deleting the shot. A skipped shot
is a record that you planned it and consciously let it go, which is a very
different thing from never having thought of it — and it is the answer when
somebody asks afterwards.

The count at the top of the section reads "2 of 7 captured".

## Things worth knowing

- **Delete shot** is inside **Details**, so it is hard to hit by accident.
- Shots belong to the shoot, not to a session, until you assign one. You can
  plan the whole list before you know which day each shot lands on.
- New shots are added to the end of the list.
