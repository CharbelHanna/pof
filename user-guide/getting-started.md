# Getting started

## Making an account

Go to the sign-up page and give a name, an email address and a password. You
are taken straight to your shoots.

Your account comes with its own private space. Nothing you put in is visible
to anyone else.

### If you forget your password

Use the forgotten-password link on the sign-in page. You will be sent an email
with a link to set a new one.

The same message is shown whether or not the address is known — that is
deliberate, so the page cannot be used to find out who has an account.

## Your first shoot

1. On the **Shoots** page, type a name into **Shoot name**. Whatever you would
   call it when talking to a client: "Chen family portraits", "Harbour at
   dawn".
2. Choose a type — Portrait, Editorial, Landscape, Product or Boudoir. This
   changes which extra details you are asked for. See
   [Shoots and sessions](shoots-and-sessions.md).
3. Press **Add shoot**.
4. Tap the shoot's name to open it.

That is enough to have something real. Everything else is optional and can be
added whenever it becomes known.

## What to fill in next

In rough order of how much difference it makes:

1. **A session** — the date and time you are actually shooting. Without one
   the shoot has no place in your diary and nothing appears on the Agenda.
2. **A [shot list](shot-lists.md)** — what you are there to get.
3. **A gear [checklist](checklists.md)** — the one that saves you when you are
   twenty minutes down the road.
4. **A [location](locations-and-light.md)** — which also gives you sunrise,
   sunset and golden hour for that spot on that date.
5. **[References](references.md)** — the images you are working from.
6. **[Crew and gear](crew-and-gear.md)** — who is coming and what is in the
   bag.

None of this is required and none of it has to be done in order.

## Finding your way around

Three places, in the menu at the top on a computer and along the bottom on a
phone:

- **Shoots** — everything you are working on.
- **Campaigns** — optional groupings of related shoots.
- **Agenda** — what is coming up, in date order.

## On the day

When you stop planning and start shooting, open the shoot and press **On
set**. See [on-set mode](on-set-mode.md).

## Using it on a phone

The whole app is built to be used on a phone — that is the point, since the
planning happens at a desk but the shooting does not. There is nothing to
install; open it in your phone's browser and sign in.
